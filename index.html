FAQ

Common questions.

How do I book a technician?

WhatsApp or call 0305 4226888. Tell us the problem and your location, and we will guide you through the booking.

Do you provide home visits?

Yes. AI Home Care is designed around home visits for the services and areas currently covered by our technicians.

Can I send a photo or video?

Yes. Sending a photo or short video on WhatsApp can help us understand the issue before the visit.

Will repair work start immediately?

The technician should first diagnose the issue and explain the required work/estimate. Repair proceeds after customer approval.

Do you provide warranty?

Where applicable, warranty/support terms are communicated for the completed service. Ask for the specific warranty terms before approving the repair.
